## \# Render Method (Meteor addon)

## 

## A Meteor Client addon with one module, \*\*Render Swap\*\*.

## 

## Press the keybind: your render distance drops to the value you set (2-32), then restores itself after a short delay (default 0.5s). No second press needed.

## 

## \## Usage

## 1\. Open the Meteor GUI (Right Shift) and find \*\*Render Swap\*\* in the Render Swap tab.

## 2\. Set `low-render-distance` (2-32) and `revert-delay-ms`.

## 3\. Right-click the module and bind a key.

## 

## \## Install

## Put the jar in `.minecraft/mods` with Meteor Client and Fabric API. Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric.

## 

## \## Build

## `./gradlew build`, jar in `build/libs/`.
